      Backup to Google Drive

      Keeps an encrypted copy of your vault in your own Drive and updates it after every change. Google only ever sees the encrypted file, so use a strong master password.

      How to get a client ID and secret
      1. In Google Cloud Console, create a project and turn on the Google Drive API.
      2. Open the OAuth consent screen, choose External, and enter an app name and your email.
      3. Publish the app. While it is in testing, Google signs you out after 7 days.
      4. Create an OAuth client of type Desktop app, then copy its client ID and secret here.
